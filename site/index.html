Connect Foundry

Add Foundry to Vestra.

  1. Copy the Foundry registry URL.
  2. In Vestra, open Extensions → Registries and add the URL.
  3. Browse Available, choose an extension, and review its permissions before installing.

For a single extension, copy its install link and paste it into Add extension. Foundry never installs or grants permissions automatically.
